Dubai

Dubai amazed me with its luxurious lifestyle, huge skyscrapers like Burj Khalifa and its futuristic vibe.

Bangalore

Bangalore, known as the Silicon Valley of India, impressed me with its pleasant climate, and its tech culture. I went with my friends and it provided a great experience.

Kashmir

Kashmir was the best trip of my life and it gave me an experience i will remember forever. The sense of peace and satisfaction i got while visiting the mountains is unmatched.
